Lemon-Garlic Chicken Thighs with Wilted Spinach

A one-pan braise that leans entirely on what you already have. The lemon goes in twice — zest at the start, juice off the heat.

18 min Serves 2 Easy 1 pan Nut-free ✓
Ingredients · you have 4 of 6
Chicken thighs, boneless in pantry
Baby spinach, 4 handfuls in pantry
Lemon — zest + juice in pantry
Garlic, 4 cloves in pantry
Olive oil, 2 tbsp need
Chilli flakes, a pinch optional
Method
1

Pat the thighs dry, season hard with salt and the lemon zest. Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium-high until it shimmers.

2

Lay the thighs in skin-side down and don't move them for 6 minutes — you want a deep gold crust. Flip, add garlic and chilli, cook 4 minutes more.

3

Push the chicken to one side, drop in the spinach in two batches and toss until just wilted, about 90 seconds.

4

Kill the heat, squeeze over the lemon juice and spoon the pan juices back over everything. Rest 2 minutes before serving.

Chef's note — no olives in your pantry, so the recipe's usual brininess is replaced by an extra squeeze of lemon at the end. Adapted from [1] and [3].
