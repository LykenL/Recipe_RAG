Chicken Wings with Cumin, Lemon & Garlic

A Turkish-style tray bake from the expanded corpus. Ten minutes of work, then the oven does the rest — marinate ahead if you can.

60 minServes 4Easy1 tray Garnish has pine nuts
Ingredients · you have 1 of 6
12 chicken wings in pantry
2 cloves garlic, minced need
Zest and juice of 1 lemon need
1 tsp. cumin seeds need
2 tbsp. olive oil need
1 tbsp. honey need
Method
1

Using sharp kitchen scissors, cut each wing at the knuckle into two pieces.

2

Mix the garlic, lemon zest and juice, cumin and oil with plenty of seasoning. Tip into a dish with the wings and toss to coat. Cover and marinate at least 1 hr, or overnight.

3

Heat oven to 200°C / 180°C fan / gas 6. Bake the wings on an oven tray for 45–50 min until crisp.

4

Drizzle the honey over for the final 10 minutes of baking. Serve on a platter with plenty of paper napkins.

Chef's note — [1] suggests serving this with pistachios or almonds, which conflicts with your nut allergy setting — leave them out. [2] was dropped for the same reason, and stays listed so the exclusion is auditable.
